a friday night in nyc versus sf

February 2, 2025

A friday night in NYC is a feeling like you are always just around the corner from the thing or feeling you couldn't find anywhere else in a very real way. One more walk or train ride away from a conversation, bar, or environmental feel that justifies the absurdity of living in such a chaotic place. Somehow it nearly always satiates, it's not a treadmill.

A friday night in SF is an aside from a more cognitive and individual state of mind, taking in for a moment the beauty of the environment and similar wavelength of the people around. Sleepy and restorative. A feeling like a perpetual secret enlightenment the peninsula is in on that the surrounding world isn't awake to yet. NYC will inevitably create the derivative of whatever that new thing is and do it in its diverse glory.
